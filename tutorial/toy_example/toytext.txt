During the stormy night, the rattle on the shelf began shaking on its own, tapping out an irregular rhythm like a warning.
Lina’s plush bear slowly lifted its stitched paw, pointing toward the dark hallway where her soft doll sat upright despite having been laid down earlier.
On the floor, her half‑finished puzzle rearranged itself piece by piece, forming an image of eyes staring back at her.
A lonely ball rolled out from under the bed, circling her feet as if urging her to follow.
Outside, the abandoned jump rope slapped against the porch in steady beats, though no wind blew.
The plush bear toppled forward, landing at her toes as its button eyes gleamed unnaturally.
Lina tried to step back, but the puzzle pieces whispered as they slid across the floor, spelling her name.
The doll’s head turned with a tiny creak, smiling a stitched smile far too wide.
Then everything went still—except for the rattle, which shook once more as if saying, “We’ve been waiting.”
